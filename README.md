Solmar is a sunlit coastal city adventure. Help Mara with five favors, take the wheel, and make your way through a changing city with the police close behind.

## Run it

- Serve the game: `node serve.ts`
- Build the entry output and dossier: `node main.ts`

Open `http://localhost:8080/` after starting the server.

## Controls

- **WASD** or **arrow keys**: walk and steer
- **Shift**: run
- **E**: enter or leave a car
- **W / S** or **up / down**: accelerate or brake
- **A / D** or **left / right**: steer
- **Space**: swing while on foot
- **P**: pause
- **Enter**: start or play again
